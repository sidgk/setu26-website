A day with Setu26

Built around your hospital's day.

Real screens from our test hospital, with sample data.
  1. MorningAvailable

    The day is ready before the desk opens

    The Today page shows the day's appointments by doctor, who is waiting for a call back and anything still owed to a patient. Doctors' shifts, leave and visit lengths are already applied, so nobody books into a gap that doesn't exist.

  2. OPD hoursAvailable

    One schedule for the desk, the phone and the app

    Reception books and moves visits in the same schedule the AI receptionist and the Swasthya app use. A time taken anywhere is gone everywhere, even when two people try at the same moment.

  3. All dayAvailable

    Every call handled. Nothing forgotten.

    Every call the receptionist takes arrives with its recording, transcript, summary and outcome. Anything it couldn't finish becomes a callback with the caller's number and the reason, in one list your team works through.

  4. At the counterAvailable

    The patient's whole story on one record

    Visits, calls, messages, documents, prescriptions and bills for that patient, with open items at the top. Staff link a patient's Swasthya app to the record by scanning a code at the desk, so documents and messages reach the right person.

  5. After the visitIn pilot

    Reports and documents, both ways

    Send a lab or scan report, a discharge summary, a prescription (typed or dictated) or a bill to the patient's app or a secure SMS link. See what patients shared with you before they arrive, and mark it reviewed.

Wards and the Staff app

From the desk to the bedside.

When a patient is admitted, the same record follows them to the ward. These parts are built and live, and we switch them on for each pilot hospital.Available to pilot hospitals

The whole hospital today

Beds by ward, inpatients, nursing tasks, housekeeping and staff on duty, in counts, never names.

The Staff app

Nurses record rounds and bedside notes bed by bed. Notes are kept on the phone without signal and sent when it returns.

Wards, rooms and beds

Set up floors, wards, rooms and beds as your hospital has them. A bed can never be given to two patients.

Admissions and transfers

Admit a patient to a bed, move them and discharge them, with every move kept in their history.

Nursing tasks

Tasks for each patient with who owns them and when they're due. Tasks from a doctor's note start only after the doctor approves them.

Shifts and handovers

Who is rostered on each ward, who actually came, and a handover note at every change of shift.

Housekeeping

A discharge creates a cleaning task. The bed is free again only after someone other than the cleaner checks it.

The hospital today

Beds by ward, inpatients, open tasks and staff on duty, on one page, in counts rather than names.

The Staff app is not yet in the app stores; we install it on your staff's phones during the pilot.

Staff and roles

Doctors see their own patients

Each doctor sees only the patients they care for. To cover for a colleague or in an emergency, they open another patient by giving a reason. That lasts twelve hours and goes in the audit log.

Available

Staff directory

Everyone who works at the hospital on one list, with their terms (permanent, contract, visiting or trainee), start and leaving dates, and the teams they belong to.

Available

Readiness for the AI receptionist

A card on your dashboard shows which records the receptionist can rely on and what to fix, such as a doctor with no profile or a specialty that doesn't match the department. Where a record is missing, it says it can't confirm rather than guess.

Works alongside what you have

Setu26 brings its own schedule and patient records, so you can start from paper or a spreadsheet. If you already use billing or hospital software, keep it. Setu26 takes care of the front desk, the wards it's switched on for and the patient connection.

Not included today

  • A full billing module (GST, insurance, packages) Planned
  • An electronic medical record or lab system Planned
  • Connection to your existing hospital software Planned

The desk can create simple bills and payment receipts and send them to the patient, but this is not a billing system.
