How it works

Three parts, working as one.

The receptionist, the hospital platform and the Swasthya app share one schedule and one patient record. Here are three everyday journeys across them, the way they work today in our test hospital.
Book a demoGetting started
Journey 01Available

A phone call becomes an appointment

  1. 1. AI receptionist

    A patient calls in Marathi while the desk is busy. The receptionist asks which doctor and when, and offers only times that are really free.

  2. 2. Appointment engine

    The chosen time is booked in the doctor's schedule. If someone took it a second earlier, the caller is offered the next free time.

  3. 3. Hospital platform

    Reception sees the booking on the calendar, with the call's summary and recording. Anything unfinished becomes a callback.

  4. 4. Swasthya

    If the patient's record is linked to Swasthya, the same visit appears in their app, where they can move or cancel it.

The AI receptionist
Journey 02In pilot

A report reaches the patient

  1. 1. Hospital platform

    After the visit, staff open the patient's record and send the lab report, or issue a prescription or a bill.

  2. 2. Patient record

    The document is stored on that patient's record, checked and kept privately in India.

  3. 3. Swasthya or SMS

    The patient finds it in their app, or opens an SMS link with their date of birth if they do not have the app.

  4. 4. Hospital platform

    Staff see whether it was delivered and opened, and can resend, correct or withdraw it.

Reports and documents
Journey 03In pilot

A patient's old report reaches the doctor

  1. 1. Swasthya

    Before a consultation, the patient photographs an earlier report and adds it to the app.

  2. 2. Patient's choice

    They share that report with the hospital, or give the doctor access to their history for a few days. The hospital can also ask for it.

  3. 3. Hospital platform

    It appears under Shared with us and on the patient's record. Opening it is recorded; staff mark it reviewed.

  4. 4. The doctor

    The doctor sees the earlier report at the visit, without the patient carrying a file.

Getting started

Live in days, not a software project.
