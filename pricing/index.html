Pilot and pricing

Simple monthly pricing, set with our first hospitals.

One monthly subscription that includes a calling allowance, with optional modules on top. We quote after a short call instead of publishing a list we may change.
See the pilotGet a quote

How pricing works

Three parts, no surprises.

01

Monthly subscription

Phone receptionist, appointment schedules, callbacks, the hospital dashboard and staff accounts, with a monthly calling allowance included.

02

Calling beyond the allowance

Charged at a rate agreed in advance and visible in your dashboard as it accrues. Larger volumes get a lower rate.

03

Optional modules

Token queue, the Swasthya patient app, doctors' calendar sync, WhatsApp messages, extra numbers or locations. Only if you want them.

Founding-hospital pilot

A 60-day pilot, measured on your own calls.

We work closely with a small number of hospitals. A pilot is a real deployment on your phone line with a clear goal agreed at the start, and an honest review at the end.

What the pilot includes

  • Setup of your doctors, timings and hospital facts with you, in a working session
  • The phone receptionist on the calls you choose: after hours, overflow, or all calls
  • The hospital dashboard for your reception and administrators
  • A test week with your staff before patients are involved
  • Direct support from the founder, with a fallback to your desk at all times
  • A review at 30 and 60 days on calls answered, bookings made and callbacks closed

What we ask of you

  • A person at the hospital who owns the pilot and answers questions within a day
  • Doctors' schedules kept up to date, including leave
  • A rough count of daily calls before we start, so we can measure the difference
  • A fixed pilot fee agreed in writing beforehand, credited towards the first year if you continue
  • Honest feedback, including what does not work
Ask about a pilot

At day 60 you continue on a monthly plan, or stop and take your data with you.

Questions

About pricing.

Why is there no price list here yet?+
We are setting prices with our first pilot hospitals, based on what calls actually cost and the value they see. Rather than publish numbers we may change, we quote clearly after a short call: one monthly fee, what it includes and what extra calls cost, before you commit to anything.
What does the price depend on?+
Mainly how many calls Setu26 handles each month, then the number of doctors and locations and the modules you switch on. A clinic with a few calls a day pays far less than a hospital with hundreds.
Is there a setup fee?+
Setting up doctors, timings and your hospital's facts takes a working session with us. For pilot hospitals there is no separate setup fee.
Do you charge per call or per minute?+
The subscription includes a monthly calling allowance. Beyond it, extra calling is charged at a rate agreed in advance and shown in your dashboard as it accrues, never as a surprise.
Is GST included?+
Prices are quoted excluding GST, which is shown separately on every quote and invoice.
Can we stop?+
Yes. A pilot can end at any time, and you keep your appointment data: we can export it for you.
