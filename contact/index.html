See Setu26 working for your hospital.

Tell us a little about your hospital. We'll reply within one working day to arrange a 30-minute call, usually by video. You phone our test hospital yourself, then see the booking, the patient record and a report arrive in a patient's app.

What happens next

  1. 1We reply by email within one working day to agree a time. Nothing is booked until then.
  2. 2On the call we learn how your desk works today, then show Setu26 booking into a sample schedule in your language and sending a sample report to a test phone.
  3. 3You get a calendar invitation once a time is agreed. If it&apos;s a fit, we agree a pilot plan in writing; if not, we&apos;ll tell you.

Not a hospital enquiry?

Patients: this form cannot book a doctor's appointment. Please call your hospital, or use the Swasthya app if your hospital offers it.

Hospitals already using Setu26: email admin@ka-26.com for support.
