Book a demo

Hear Setu26 answer a call for your hospital.

Tell us a little about your hospital. We reply within one working day to arrange a 30-minute call, usually by video, where you phone our test hospital yourself.

What happens next

  1. 1We reply by email within one working day to agree a time. Nothing is booked until then.
  2. 2On the call we learn how your desk handles calls today, then show Setu26 booking into a sample schedule in your language.
  3. 3You get a calendar invitation once a time is agreed. If it is a fit, we agree a pilot plan in writing; if not, we will tell you.

Not a hospital enquiry?

Patients: this form cannot book a doctor's appointment. Please call your hospital, or use the Swasthya app if your hospital offers it.

Hospitals already using Setu26: email admin@ka-26.com for support.

Please do not send patient information

This form is for your hospital's business enquiry only. Do not include any patient's name, phone number or medical details.
