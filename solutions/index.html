Solutions

Start with the phone. Add the rest when you need it.

Most hospitals begin with the phone receptionist and the appointment schedule behind it. Modules switch on when they help, and you pay for what you use.

The core

Answering and booking.

Available

Phone receptionist

For calls that go unanswered when the desk is busy or closed.

Answers in Kannada, Marathi, Hindi or English, including callers who mix them. Books, moves and cancels visits, and answers common questions from facts you control: OPD timings, which doctor sits when, fees, directions.

When a caller mentions an emergency, it gives your emergency instructions at once. It never gives medical advice.

Available

Appointments and schedules

For double bookings and confusion about who is free.

One schedule per doctor with shifts, leave and visit lengths. Phone, desk and patient app all book against it, and a slot can be given only once, even when two people try at the same moment.

Available

Callbacks and handoff

For AI that guesses, or promises what it cannot do.

If a booking cannot be completed or a caller asks for a person, Setu26 transfers the call or records a callback with the reason and the caller's number. Your staff see a clear list of what is waiting.

Available

Hospital dashboard

For no visibility into what happens on the phone.

Every call with its recording, transcript, summary and outcome. Appointments, the day calendar by doctor, callbacks, patients and simple reports on calls answered and bookings made.

Staff accounts with roles, so a receptionist sees what a receptionist needs.

Modules

Switch on what helps.

In pilot

Doctors' own calendars

Connect a doctor's Google Calendar or Outlook. Bookings appear there with the patient's name and visit type, and personal events block those times. Only the times are read, never the details.

Optional module

Token queue

Numbered tokens per doctor and session, with a limit, a share kept for walk-ins, a desk board and a waiting-room screen.

Optional module

Swasthya patient app

An Android app in English, Hindi, Kannada and Marathi for booking, tokens and records your hospital shares. Same schedule as the phone and desk.

PlannedConnecting to existing hospital software (HIS) is planned, not available yet.
