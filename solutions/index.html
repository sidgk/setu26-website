All capabilities

Everything Setu26 does, and how ready each part is.

Every capability carries one of five labels. Available: running in our live platform. In pilot: built and tested end to end, offered to pilot hospitals, not yet used by a hospital with real patients. Early access: usable, with the limits stated. Coming soon: built, being switched on. Planned: not built yet.

Hospital platform

The hospital platform

  • Appointments and calendar

    One schedule per doctor with shifts, leave and visit lengths; a slot is never given twice.

    Available

  • Today page and callbacks

    The day's appointments, callers waiting for a call back and open items.

    Available

  • Patient records

    Visits, calls, messages, documents, prescriptions and bills on one record.

    Available

  • Staff accounts, roles and two-step sign-in

    Roles for owner, administrator, reception and doctors; custom roles.

    Available

  • Doctors see only their own patients

    Another patient opens with a reason, for twelve hours, and goes in the audit log.

    Available

  • Staff directory

    Terms, start and leaving dates, and teams for everyone who works at the hospital.

    Available

  • Readiness for the AI receptionist

    A dashboard card shows which records the receptionist can rely on and what to fix.

    Available

  • Your hospital's own web address

    For example your-hospital.ka-26.com, with a separate sign-in per hospital.

    Available

  • Audit log and reports

    Who changed or opened what; calls, bookings and callbacks over time.

    Available

  • Token queue

    Tokens per doctor and session, walk-in share, desk board and waiting-room screen.

    Optional module

  • Doctors' Google Calendar and Outlook

    Bookings appear in the doctor's calendar; personal events block those times. Only busy times are read.

    Coming soon

  • Full billing module, EMR, lab system

    Not part of Setu26 today.

    Planned

  • Connection to existing hospital software

    Interfaces are designed; no connection is available yet.

    Planned

AI receptionist

The AI receptionist

  • Answers in Kannada, Marathi, Hindi and English

    Including mixed speech; only from facts your hospital controls.

    Available

  • Books, moves and cancels appointments

    Into real free times in the same schedule as the desk and the app.

    Available

  • Callbacks and handoff

    Anything it can't finish becomes a callback with the reason.

    Available

  • Emergency instructions

    Gives your emergency instructions straight away and stops booking.

    Available

  • Recordings, transcripts and summaries

    For every call, with retention you set.

    Available

  • Live transfer to your desk or emergency number

    Tested on our test line; being set up on Indian numbers.

    Coming soon

  • Indian phone number for your hospital

    Being arranged with an Indian carrier.

    Coming soon

One schedule

Taken anywhere. Gone everywhere.

The receptionist, your staff and the patient app read and write the same appointment record. That's what lets Setu26 confirm a booking instead of taking a message.

See it working with your own doctors.

In a 30-minute call, we set up two of your doctors in our test hospital. Call it yourself in Kannada, Marathi, Hindi or English, then watch the booking, the patient record and a report arrive in the patient's app.
