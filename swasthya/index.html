Swasthya patient app

The patient's side of your hospital.

Swasthya connects patients to the hospitals that use Setu26: the same schedule, the same record and the same documents your staff see, on the patient's phone, in English, Hindi, Kannada or Marathi.

How it connects to your hospital

Linked to your record, not a separate system.

  1. 01

    The patient signs in with their phone number

    A one-time code by SMS. No password to remember.

  2. 02

    They connect to your hospital at the desk

    They scan your code, or show theirs, and your staff confirm the patient record. Only then do documents and messages flow.

  3. 03

    Everything else runs on your platform

    Their bookings are in your schedule, their tokens in your queue, their messages and shared documents on their record.

AI, only with the patient's consent

Swasthya's AI features are off until the patient turns them on, and they can turn them off again in Settings. With AI on, when a patient adds a document, the app sends it to Google's Gemini AI service to suggest its type, title and date for them to check. Today we use Google's free tier, whose terms allow Google to use the content to improve its products. Patients can also ask the app health questions; the answers come from the same service and are not medical advice.

Patients can correct or clear anything the AI suggests before saving. Every hospital is told about this, with our full list of providers, before a pilot starts.

For patients

Swasthya works only with hospitals that use Setu26 and have switched it on. If yours isn't one of them, please call your hospital as usual. This website can't book appointments, and your records belong to your hospital, so please contact them with any questions.
