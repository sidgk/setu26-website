For patients

Swasthya, the patient app that comes with Setu26.

If your hospital uses Setu26 and offers Swasthya, you can book visits, take a token, message the hospital, receive your reports and share earlier ones, in English, Hindi, Kannada or Marathi.
What Swasthya does

Book with your hospital

Free times come from the hospital's own schedule.

Take a token

Where your hospital runs a token queue.

Your reports in one place

What your hospital sends you, and the earlier reports you choose to share.

This website can't book appointments

Please call your hospital as usual. If it uses Setu26, your call may be answered by Setu26, which can book you into the same schedule. We can't book for hospitals that don't use Setu26. Your records belong to your hospital, so please contact them with any questions.

Getting the app

Swasthya is for Android phones and is installed from a link your hospital gives you. It is not on Google Play or the iPhone App Store yet.
