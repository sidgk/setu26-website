The front desk problem

Your reception desk cannot answer every call, and every unanswered call is a patient who may go elsewhere

During OPD hours the line is engaged while staff handle the queue in front of them. After hours nobody picks up. The same questions about timings, fees and which doctor sits on which day come in all day, often in a language your staff are not comfortable in. Setu26 takes the routine calls so your team can look after the people standing in front of them.

What Setu26 does

A receptionist on the phone that books, not just talks

Speaks your patients' language

Callers speak Kannada, Marathi, Hindi or English, or mix them, and Setu26 answers in kind. It answers from your hospital's own facts: timings, doctors, fees, directions.

Books into your real schedule

It offers only times your doctors are actually free and confirms the booking in the same schedule your desk and the patient app use, so the same slot can never be given twice.

Hands off when it should

When a caller needs a person, an emergency is mentioned or a booking cannot be completed, Setu26 transfers the call or records a callback for your staff, with the reason, instead of making a promise it cannot keep.

How it works

From a ringing phone to a confirmed appointment your staff can see

  1. 1A patient calls your numberCalls you choose (all, after hours, or when the desk is busy) reach Setu26. It greets them with your hospital's name.
  2. 2Setu26 finds a real timeIt asks what they need, checks the doctor's schedule, leave and existing bookings, and offers free times.
  3. 3The booking is confirmedThe appointment appears on your dashboard and calendar immediately. If the doctor uses Google Calendar or Outlook, it appears there too.
  4. 4Your team sees everythingEach call has a recording, a transcript and a short summary. Anything Setu26 could not finish is waiting as a callback.

Who it's for

Built for owner-run hospitals and busy clinics

Setu26 fits best where the phone rings more than the desk can handle and appointments matter: multispecialty clinics and small to medium private hospitals with several doctors, especially where many patients prefer Kannada or Marathi.

  • Three to thirty doctors with set OPD timings
  • A reception desk that misses calls at peak hours or after closing
  • Appointments kept on paper, a spreadsheet or a simple system, with no complex software to integrate
  • Patients who would rather speak Kannada, Marathi or Hindi
