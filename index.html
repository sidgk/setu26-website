Privacy and control

Control stays with your hospital.

Your staff decide

Your team sees every call, booking and document, can step in at any point, and can switch the receptionist off in one step.

Stored in India

Databases, recordings and documents live in Google Cloud's Mumbai region. Some call and AI processing by our providers happens abroad; we name each one.

Every hospital separate

The database itself keeps each hospital's patients apart. Patients see only their own records and choose what they share.

No medical advice

Setu26 books, answers administrative questions and moves documents. It does not interpret reports or give medical advice.

Setu26 does not hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 or HIPAA certification, and is not connected to ABDM. Security in detail

Questions

What hospitals ask us.

Do we have to replace our hospital software?+
No. Setu26 brings its own schedule, patient records and document sharing, so a hospital running on paper, a register or spreadsheets can start straight away, and a hospital with billing or HIS software keeps it. Setu26 is not a full hospital information system or an electronic medical record. Connecting to existing hospital software is planned, not yet available.
Can patients share their earlier reports with us?+
Yes, through Swasthya. A patient uploads a report and shares it with your hospital, or gives your doctor time-limited access to their history. Your staff see it on the patient's record and mark it reviewed. Built, tested and offered in pilots. How documents move
What if a patient doesn't use the app?+
Your staff can still send a report or bill as an SMS link that opens only with the patient's date of birth. And patients can keep phoning: the receptionist books into the same schedule as the app and the desk.
Do we keep our phone number?+
Yes. Patients keep calling the number they know, and you decide which calls reach Setu26: all of them, after hours, or when the line is busy, usually by call forwarding. The Indian number Setu26 answers on is being arranged with an Indian carrier; we confirm the date before a pilot starts.
What happens when the receptionist can't finish?+
It never pretends. If a time has just been taken, it offers the nearest free ones. If it can't complete a booking, or the caller asks for a person, it leaves your staff a callback with the reason. Live transfer to your desk is being set up on Indian numbers.
How long does setup take?+
One working session with us to enter your doctors, timings and hospital details, then a test week with your staff before any patient is involved.
What does it cost?+
A monthly subscription with a calling allowance included, plus optional modules. We are setting prices with our first hospitals, so we quote after a short call. Pilots run for 60 days at a fixed fee agreed in writing. How pricing works
