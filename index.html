Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do we keep our existing phone number?+
Yes. Patients keep calling the number they know. You choose which calls reach Setu26 (all, after hours, or when the line is busy), usually by call forwarding from your number. Setting up the Indian number for your pilot is part of onboarding.
Do we have to replace our hospital software?+
No. Setu26 brings its own appointment schedule, so hospitals that use paper, a register or a spreadsheet can start straight away. Connecting to existing hospital software is planned, not available yet.
What happens when the AI cannot finish?+
It never pretends. If a time was just taken it offers the nearest free ones; if it cannot complete a booking, or the caller wants a person, it transfers the call or records a callback with the reason for your staff.
Can our receptionists step in?+
Always. Your team sees every call as it happens and afterwards, books and changes appointments in the same schedule, and works through callbacks. You can switch Setu26 off in one step.
How long does setup take?+
One working session with us to enter your doctors, timings and hospital facts, then a test week with your staff before any patient is involved.
What does it cost?+
A monthly subscription that includes a calling allowance, with optional modules. We are setting prices with our first hospitals, so we quote after a short call. Pilots run for 60 days at a fixed fee agreed in writing. How pricing works
How is patient information protected?+
Data is stored in India, each hospital is kept separate at the database level, and our staff cannot open patient records without your approval. Security and privacy
