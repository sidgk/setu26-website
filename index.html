How documents move

From the hospital to the patient

In pilot
  1. 1

    Staff choose the patient and the document

    A lab or scan report, discharge summary, certificate, a prescription (typed or dictated) or a bill. A check box confirms it is the right patient.

  2. 2

    It is added to that patient's record

    Stored privately in India. Files are checked by their contents and size before they are accepted.

  3. 3

    The patient receives it

    In their Swasthya app, or as an SMS link that opens only with their date of birth and expires in 7 days.

  4. 4

    Staff see what happened

    In the app, notified, opened. Resend, correct with a new version, or withdraw; the patient is told.

From the patient to the hospital

In pilot
  1. 1

    The patient adds their documents

    Photos, scans or PDFs of earlier reports, in Swasthya. They are always labelled as uploaded by the patient.

  2. 2

    They choose what to share, and for how long

    Specific documents with one connected hospital, or time-limited access to their history. They can stop it at any time.

  3. 3

    The hospital can ask

    Staff can request a document or history access; the patient decides.

  4. 4

    Staff review it

    Shared documents appear under Shared with us and on the patient's record. Opening one is recorded; staff mark it reviewed.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

Do we have to replace our hospital software?+
No. Setu26 brings its own appointment schedule, patient records and document sharing, so hospitals on paper, a register or a spreadsheet can start straight away, and hospitals with billing or HIS software keep it. Setu26 is not a full hospital information system or an electronic medical record. Connecting to existing hospital software is planned, not available yet.
Can patients send us their old reports?+
Yes, through the Swasthya app: a patient uploads a report and shares it with your hospital, or gives your doctor time-limited access to their history. Your staff see it on the patient's record and mark it reviewed. This is built and tested and offered in pilots. How documents move
What if a patient does not have the app?+
Your staff can still send them a report or bill: they get an SMS link that opens only with their date of birth. They can also keep phoning: the receptionist books into the same schedule as the app and the desk.
Do we keep our existing phone number?+
Yes. Patients keep calling the number they know, and you choose which calls reach Setu26 (all, after hours, or when the line is busy), usually by call forwarding. An Indian number for Setu26 to answer on is being arranged with an Indian carrier; we confirm the date before a pilot starts.
What happens when the AI cannot finish?+
It never pretends. If a time was just taken it offers the nearest free ones; if it cannot complete a booking, or the caller wants a person, it records a callback with the reason for your staff (live transfer to your desk is being set up on Indian numbers).
How long does setup take?+
One working session with us to enter your doctors, timings and hospital facts, then a test week with your staff before any patient is involved.
What does it cost?+
A monthly subscription that includes a calling allowance, with optional modules. We are setting prices with our first hospitals, so we quote after a short call. Pilots run for 60 days at a fixed fee agreed in writing. How pricing works
