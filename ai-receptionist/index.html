When things go wrong

It never pretends. A failure ends with a person following up.

A receptionist that fails quietly is worse than none. For each case, here is what the patient hears and what your team sees.

The time was just taken

The patient hears
"Sorry, 10:30 has just been booked. I can offer 11:00 or 12:30 tomorrow."
Your team sees
The booking at the new time, as normal. A slot is never given to two people.

A system is unavailable

The patient hears
"I can't confirm this right now. Someone from the hospital will call you back today."
Your team sees
A callback with the caller's number, the doctor and time they wanted.

The caller wants a person

The patient hears
"Let me connect you to the front desk."
Your team sees
The transferred call (being set up on Indian numbers), or a callback with the reason.

The call ends midway

The patient hears
Nothing more: they hung up.
Your team sees
A callback if they had chosen a time but not finished, so your team can complete it.

An emergency is mentioned

The patient hears
Your hospital's emergency instructions, or a transfer to your emergency number, straight away. Booking stops.
Your team sees
The call flagged for attention, with the transcript.

Too many people call at once

The patient hears
"The hospital will call you back shortly."
Your team sees
A callback for each caller beyond our line's capacity (being set up on Indian numbers).

Your hospital's role

You stay in control.

Setting up is one working session with us, not a software project. You do not need existing hospital software. Then a test week with your staff before any patient calls.

  • Doctors and OPD timings

    Names, departments, days and hours, leave and visit length. Change them any time; Setu26 follows straight away.

  • What callers are told

    Timings, fees, address, what to bring and your emergency instructions. Setu26 answers only from these facts.

  • Which calls it answers

    All calls, after hours only, or when the desk is busy, and the number to transfer to.

  • Who sees what

    Accounts for reception and administrators, each with the access their role needs.

What it does not do

  • It does not give medical advice, triage or interpret reports.
  • It does not ask about diagnoses or medical history. For a booking it needs the patient's name and a short reason; before talking about reports or bills it can confirm identity, for example with a date of birth.
  • It does not replace your staff: anything that needs a person goes to a person.

Part of a larger platform

Bookings it makes appear on the same calendar your reception uses and in patients' Swasthya app. A caller who asks about a report can be helped by your staff, who send it to the patient's app or by a secure SMS link.
