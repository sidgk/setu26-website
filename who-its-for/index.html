Who it's for

Built for hospitals where the phone never stops.

We would rather tell you now if Setu26 is not a good fit than after a pilot. Here is where it works well today.

A good fit today

Owner-run hospitals and busy clinics.

Multispecialty clinics and polyclinics

Three to fifteen doctors with set OPD timings, where patients phone to ask who is available and to book.

Small and medium private hospitals

A busy OPD and a reception desk that cannot keep up with calls at peak hours or after closing.

High-demand single specialties

Paediatrics, gynaecology, orthopaedics, ENT, dermatology and similar, where booking calls are most of the phone traffic.

Regional-language patients

Especially in Karnataka and Maharashtra, where many callers would rather speak Kannada or Marathi than English.

Signs it will help

Does this sound like your desk?

  • Patients say they could not get through on the phone
  • Your receptionist answers the same questions all day
  • Calls after OPD hours go unanswered
  • Appointments live on paper, a register or a spreadsheet
  • Doctors' leave and schedule changes cause clashes
  • Staff struggle with callers who speak another language

Not a good fit yet

Who should wait.

Setu26 is young. These hospitals will be better served once integrations and certifications that do not exist today are in place.

  • Bookings must live in your existing hospital software

    Setu26 keeps its own appointment schedule. Connecting to existing software is planned, not available.

  • Formal certification is required before a pilot

    We do not hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 today. Our security page explains the measures we do have.

  • Telemedicine, triage or clinical advice

    Setu26 books appointments and answers administrative questions. It does not give medical advice.

  • Other languages

    Languages beyond Kannada, Marathi, Hindi and English are not supported yet.

Not sure if you fit?

Tell us about your hospital in a short call. If Setu26 is not right for you yet, we will say so.
