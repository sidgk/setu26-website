About Setu26

A bridge between patients and the hospitals that care for them.

Setu means bridge. We build the connection a patient needs to reach their hospital, by phone or by app, in the language they think in, at any hour, without being left on hold.

Why we exist

Care still begins with a phone call.

When the line is engaged, nobody answers after hours, or the person who picks up does not speak their language, a call can end without an appointment, and the patient may go elsewhere or not go at all.

Hospitals know this. Their front desks do their best with a queue in front of them and the phone ringing behind them. They do not need another complicated system. They need the phone answered properly and a schedule they can trust.

What we believe

Four principles we build by.

01

Answer every patient in their language

Kannada, Marathi, Hindi and English first, because a patient who has to switch languages to book a visit has already been let down.

02

Book, do not just talk

An AI that chats but cannot confirm a real appointment adds work. Setu26 books into the same schedule your staff use, or hands over honestly.

03

Support staff, do not replace judgement

Setu26 takes the repetitive calls so your team can care for the people in front of them. Anything that needs a person goes to a person.

04

Earn trust with evidence

We say what is ready and what is not, measure results on a hospital's own calls, and never claim outcomes we have not shown.

Where we are

Early, focused, and honest about it.

Setu26 is an early-stage company registered in Gadag, Karnataka, built by a founder who designs, builds and supports the product directly. The hospital platform, the AI receptionist, the Swasthya patient app and two-way document sharing work end to end in our test hospital; the receptionist has handled hundreds of test calls in four languages. We are looking for our first pilot hospitals in Karnataka and Maharashtra.

Our ambition is one connected way for patients to reach their hospital and for hospitals to see every request and every document, whether it arrives by phone, app or at the desk. Billing, connections to existing hospital software and more will be added as they become real, one hospital at a time.
